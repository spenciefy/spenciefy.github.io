My drawing is taking a while.

A doodle usually takes about 60–90 seconds. If you left the app, reopen it with an internet connection to check for the finished drawing.

Where are my doodles?

Your collection is on your phone. To keep a copy in your camera roll, open a doodle and tap Save. You can also turn on auto-save in Settings.

Where’s my purchase?

Open the doodle shop while signed in with the same Apple account you used in lil doodles. If the balance still looks wrong, email me the pack size and approximate purchase time so I can help.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings in lil doodles, choose Delete account, and confirm with Apple. This removes your account and unused doodles. Your saved collection stays on your phone.
