## Design Context
Users: people discovering lil doodles through a shared link who want to turn personal moments, family, pets, and travel photos into marker illustrations.
Brand: warm, handmade, playful. Established raspberry logo, cream paper, Chewy lettering from the app, tilted doodle prints.
Direction: match the first two scrapbook App Store graphics without embedding screenshots. Prints on sides, clear central logo and 1-to-2 diagram. Minimal copy, one App Store action. Keep support/privacy accessible and preserve OG metadata. Responsive layout and reduced motion required.
